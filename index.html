Exterior view — photography: Masano Kawana

A conservation shophouse in Singapore, reworked as a private residence. The building presents two distinct faces: a restored heritage facade to the street, and a new courtyard elevation opened entirely to water and sky.

The design negotiates between preservation and intervention — every addition legible as contemporary, every retained element left intact. The water court at the centre of the plan mediates light, ventilation, and the experience of moving through the house.

A Courtyard House

A three-generation family inherited a solid, dim house and needed it entirely rethought. A decent pool, a garden, a koi pond and a hobby room — the brief was domestic and specific.

Three hierarchic blocks arranged in a C-shape emerged around a central courtyard. Light enters through voids punched through ceilings and walls, coloured in different shades drawn from Le Corbusier's palette. The linkages between blocks — staircases, bridges, hallways — were crafted to create unexpected moments of living.
